# Glossary

## Refund
Money returned to a customer for an order they paid for.

## Order
A customer purchase in the orders system.

## Recent order
An order placed in the last 30 days.

## Support agent
A support user who handles customer refund requests.

## Eligible refund
A refund for an order that is recent and has not already been refunded.

## Partial refund
Returning only part of the money for an order.
